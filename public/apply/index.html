How it works

Who may read it
Members who say they're accredited and whose check size fits your round
Why

Your application is not public. It goes to members who have stated in writing that they're accredited and will keep it confidential, and whose check size fits your round. Stag Hunt's admins can open it too.

A call
A member may read your application and call you
Why

A real conversation can find what a form misses, in both directions.

Then
If that member shares it with the group, members it fits may say yes or no, each alone
Why

When answers are independent, one skeptic or one fan has less sway over the rest.

Cost
Nothing. No fund, no fees.
Why

We take nothing from founders, and members may not take pay or perks from a company for bringing its deal.

Or just talk

Skip the typing: pitch out loud to our AI interviewer in ten to fifteen minutes. It fills in the form from what you say, and you check it before sending. No video needed. Members may listen to the recording.

Connecting

Uses your microphone. By starting, you agree to be recorded. If you send your application, members whose check size fits can read and listen to it. How we handle it

A member may take the call. If they share your company with the group, members it fits may say yes or no, and each decides alone whether to invest, in their own name. There is no fund and nothing to pay. Applying is not a promise of a call or of investment.

Stag Hunt is a new way to invest: investors share every good early-stage deal they find, and each decides alone. It is not a fund, broker-dealer, funding portal or investment adviser, and it does not offer, sell or recommend securities. It never pools, holds or moves money. Neither Stag Hunt nor its organizer or members take fees or compensation from any company discussed. Members' calls and comments are opinions, not investment advice. Every investment is made directly between a member and a company, on that company's own terms and documents. Startup investing is illiquid and high-risk; most startups fail.
