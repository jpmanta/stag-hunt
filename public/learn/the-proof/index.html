The stag hunter goes hungry and switches

The hunt settles in one of two places: all on the stag, or all on hares. The stag is better for both, yet no one has to be greedy to end up with hare. Expectations decide it.

How sure do you need to be?

Hunting the stag is a bet on your partner. If they show, you feast. If not, you go hungry. A hare pays the same either way.

With these numbers, hunt the stag only if you are more than 25% sure your partner will.

Trust spreads, and so does doubt

Picture a crowd that copies whatever worked last time. Above 25% on the stag, the stag pays, and the crowd joins until everyone hunts it. Below that line, it fades until everyone takes hares.
