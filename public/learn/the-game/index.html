Chapter I

The Game

A hare in the hand, or a stag in the bush

In 1755, Rousseau described a hunt that still explains how people work together.

If a deer was to be taken, every one saw that, in order to succeed, he must abide faithfully by his post: but if a hare happened to come within the reach of any one of them, it is not to be doubted that he pursued it without scruple.Rousseau, 1755

The same choice runs through teams, markets and startup investing.

Who eats what

Half a stag feeds you for 4 days. A hare feeds you for 1. Chasing the stag alone gets you 0. Half a stag beats a hare, and a hare beats nothing: that order makes it a stag hunt.

Neither Stag Hunt nor its organizer or members take fees or compensation from any company discussed. Members' calls and comments are opinions, not investment advice. Every investment is made directly between a member and a company, on that company's own terms and documents. Startup investing is illiquid and high-risk; most startups fail.
