More hunters, more trust needed

Now the stag needs everyone. Two reliable people will usually both show. Ten reliable people will often be missing one.

How reliable each member must be: 25% with two, 71% with five, 86% with ten, 91% with fifteen.

Every hunter a deal depends on is one more who might not show.

The fix is a hunt where no deal needs everyone.

Four ways to catch the stag

The obstacle is not greed. It is doubt. Remove the doubt and the stag falls.

The prize is real. In seed investing, a few deals make nearly all the money. The more good deals you see, the better your odds of one.

4%of 21,000 US seed rounds returned 10x or more

These odds treat each deal as an average seed round, with the same 4% chance. Real deals differ, and seeing a deal is not owning a share of it.

Alone, we catch hares. Together, we catch the stag.
