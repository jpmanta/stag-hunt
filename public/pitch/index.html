Stag Hunt is a new way to invest: investors share every good early-stage deal they find, and each decides alone. It is not a fund, broker-dealer, funding portal or investment adviser, and it does not offer, sell or recommend securities. It never pools, holds or moves money. Neither Stag Hunt nor its organizer or members take fees or compensation from any company discussed. Members' calls and comments are opinions, not investment advice. Every investment is made directly between a member and a company, on that company's own terms and documents. Startup investing is illiquid and high-risk; most startups fail.
