Investing is your decision and your risk

Startup investments are risky and illiquid, and most fail. Only invest money you can afford to lose entirely. You are responsible for deciding whether to invest and for your own due diligence.

Check that you're eligible before you invest. Most private rounds are open only to accredited investors: generally, people with $200,000 in yearly income ($300,000 with a spouse or partner), $1 million in net worth not counting their home, or a Series 7, 65 or 82 license. Each company decides who may invest and checks it under its own documents. Stag Hunt does not verify anyone's eligibility.

Your investor statement. Before you see any deal, you tell us in writing whether you're an accredited investor and on what basis, and you promise to keep deals confidential. We show deals only to members who say they're accredited. If you joined on or after November 19, 2026, deals appear 30 days after your statement, so that every round reaches only people Stag Hunt already knows. Your statement must be true, and you must update it in your profile if it changes. A false statement is a breach of these terms and ends your membership. Members who aren't accredited can still bring deals, start hunts and keep a profile.

Conditional pledges ("The hunt") are a way for members to signal and coordinate interest with each other. A pledge is a promise you make to the other members. It is not a legal contract with us or with anyone else, and not an agreement to buy any security. Any actual investment happens only when you sign the company's own documents. Keeping your pledges is a matter of trust, and it shows on your profile.

House rules

  • Confidentiality. What's shared in Stag Hunt stays in Stag Hunt. Don't share deals, materials, members' identities or discussions outside the group without permission.
  • Disclose your interest. Say if you work at, advise, have invested in, or would benefit from any company you post or discuss.
  • Be accurate. Don't post anything false or misleading. Say clearly what you know firsthand and what you heard.
  • No selling. Don't use Stag Hunt to raise money for yourself, charge members, or promote paid services without an admin's approval.
  • Get the founder's OK first. Only post a deal if the founder has agreed to share it with the group. Only share materials you're allowed to share, and treat founders' information with care.
  • Clean deals only. Don't post companies that make money from gambling or betting, pornography or other adult content, or cheating or deceiving people (for example, tools for cheating on schoolwork or in relationships, fake reviews, or scams). Admins remove any deal that breaks this rule.

No member, including the organizer, may accept money, equity, advisory shares, warrants, a discounted allocation or anything else of value from a company for bringing its deal to the group or for members' investments.
