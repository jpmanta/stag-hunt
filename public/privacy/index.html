Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy explains what Stag Hunt collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect only what the group needs to work, show it only to members, and never sell it or use it for ads.

What we collect

  • Your investor statement. Whether you're an accredited investor and on what basis, the promises you agreed to, and when. Only admins can see it.
  • Account details. Your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address, name and the fact that the email is verified. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • Your profile. Anything you choose to add: photo, headline, location, bio, networks, sectors, typical check size, and LinkedIn, X or website links.
  • What you post. Deals, materials links, your yes or no calls and reasons, comments, chat messages, pledges, investment amounts you log, and outcomes.
  • Sign-in data. For Face ID / Touch ID, we store a public key for each device. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device. Email sign-in codes and links are stored only in scrambled form and expire after 10 minutes.
  • Basic technical data. A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in. Your IP address, kept for about an hour to block abuse. Standard server logs from our hosting provider.

We don't use advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To run the group: sign you in, show deals and discussions to members, keep the members list, and show reputation (deals brought, calls made, pledges kept, good calls).
  • To send emails you'd expect: sign-in links, new-deal alerts and the weekly digest (you can turn both off in your profile), mention notifications, and admin notices such as account deletion requests.
  • To keep the site safe and working, and to make monthly backups of the group's deal records, which are sent to admins.

Who can see it

  • Members and observers can see your profile, what you post, and your reputation. Observers can read but are hidden from the members list, apart from a line naming them.
  • The public sees none of it. Public pages never show members, companies, deal terms or results.
  • Service providers. The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database, email delivery, and the speech-to-text and AI models used for recorded calls). Founders' spoken interviews run on ElevenLabs (voice and conversation). If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles that step. They process data only to provide these services.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We never sell your personal information.

Recorded founder calls

A member can record a conversation with a founder on their phone, or send the founder a link to record a pitch on their own phone, so the deal fills itself in. Here is exactly what happens:

  • Consent first. In person, the member must tell the founder they're recording and confirm the founder agreed before recording starts. With a link, the founder agrees on their own screen before anything records.
  • Links are private and short-lived. Only someone with the link can open it, and it stops working after 7 days, once the pitch is sent, or when the member removes it.
  • Audio is never saved. Each minute of sound is sent to Cloudflare's speech-to-text service, turned into text, and discarded.
  • The transcript is private. Only the member who recorded it, or who sent the link, can see it. It's deleted when they post the deal, when they discard the draft, or after 7 days, whichever comes first.
  • AI drafts, a person checks. An AI model on Cloudflare drafts the deal's answers from the transcript. It never scores deals. The member checks every answer before posting, and the deal says it was drafted from a recorded call.
  • Recordings and transcripts are never used to train AI models or shared outside the service.

Founder applications

Founders can apply at staghunts.com/apply. We keep what you send so members can read it. Only signed-in members who have stated they're accredited investors, and whose check size fits your round, see your application, and only the member who takes your call sees your email address. We email you once to confirm we got it. If a member shares your company with the group, your answers become part of that deal's record. Ask us any time to delete your application.

AI pitch interviews

Founders can pitch out loud to an AI interviewer instead of typing. It speaks in Jonathan Manta's voice and says it's an AI. Here is exactly what happens:

  • You choose it. The interview starts only when you press Start and allow your microphone. Starting it means you agree to be recorded. You can stop at any time and use the typed form instead.
  • It is recorded. ElevenLabs runs the conversation and keeps the audio and transcript on our account. We keep the transcript, its length and its cost.
  • AI drafts, you check. An AI model on Cloudflare drafts your application from the transcript. It never judges or scores your company. You check every answer before you send.
  • Who hears it. Only if you send the application: signed-in members whose check size fits can read the transcript and listen to the recording. If you don't send it, no member sees it.
  • Deleting it. Ask us and we delete the recording and transcript from Stag Hunt and ElevenLabs.
  • Interviews are never used to train AI models by us, and never sold or shared outside these services.

We also keep a scrambled version of your IP address with each interview to stop abuse.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you're a member. If you leave or are removed, your sign-ins are deleted right away. Deals, calls and messages you posted may stay in the group's record so the history makes sense. You can ask us to delete or anonymize your profile and personal details. Your investor statements are the one exception: we keep each one as it was made, because it is the record of who was allowed to see deals.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove profile details and your photo at any time in your profile.
  • Turn email alerts on or off in your profile.
  • Edit or delete your own chat messages.
  • Delete your account from your profile, on the web or in the iPhone app ("Delete account"). We remove it within 7 days and email you when it's done.
  • Contact us to get a copy of your data or correct it.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Session tokens and sign-in codes are stored only in hashed form, and passkeys keep your biometrics on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us right away if you think your account has been misused.

Children

Stag Hunt is for adults only. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if our practices change, and tell members about significant changes. Questions or requests? Send us a message. See also the Terms of Service.
